MEDICAL OFFICER — EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT
KPJ Hospitals · Sample job description for demonstration

Requirements
- MBBS or equivalent medical degree.
- Malaysian Medical Council (MMC) full registration.
- Current Annual Practising Certificate (APC).
- Minimum 3 years of clinical experience.
- Basic Life Support (BLS) certification.
- Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS) certification.
- Experience in emergency medicine, clinical assessment and clinical documentation.
- Knowledge of patient safety and multidisciplinary teamwork.

Preferred
- Electronic medical records (EMR) experience.
- Quality improvement experience.

Responsibilities
Assess and manage patients, coordinate specialist referrals and support safe emergency department care.
