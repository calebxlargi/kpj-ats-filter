STAFF NURSE — MEDICAL & SURGICAL WARD
KPJ Hospitals · Sample job description for demonstration

Requirements
- Diploma in Nursing or Bachelor of Nursing.
- Malaysian Nursing Board registration.
- Current Annual Practising Certificate (APC).
- Minimum 3 years of clinical experience.
- Basic Life Support (BLS) certification.
- Experience in patient assessment, medication administration and wound care.
- Knowledge of infection control and patient safety.

Preferred
- Electronic medical records (EMR) experience.
- Patient education and multidisciplinary teamwork.

Responsibilities
Provide compassionate bedside care, maintain accurate clinical documentation and support safe handovers.
