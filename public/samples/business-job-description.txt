BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT & MARKETING EXECUTIVE
KPJ Hospitals · Sample job description for demonstration

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business Administration or a related discipline.
- Minimum 3 years of relevant experience.
- Experience in business development and healthcare marketing.
- Corporate partnerships and stakeholder management experience.
- Ability to conduct market research and prepare sales reporting.
- Experience with CRM and digital marketing.

Preferred
- Event management and campaign management experience.
- Budget management experience.

Responsibilities
Grow corporate healthcare relationships, coordinate marketing campaigns and report on business performance.
